# ReachED

**Building data systems for better education access in the Philippines.**

We are a data engineering capstone team in the FTW Data Engineering program.

Education data in the Philippines is spread across multiple government agencies. That makes it hard to see where access to education and capacity fall short. Our working theme asks:

> Where are the largest gaps in education access and capacity, and which provinces or municipalities should be prioritized for additional schools, classrooms, teachers, or higher-education capacity?

> 🚧 **Early stage.** We are still discovering sources and scoping the business question. Nothing here is a finding yet.

## How we work

- **Evidence before answers.** The business question comes from what education planners need to decide *and* what the data can validly support.
- **Inspect before trusting.** Every dataset is inventoried and profiled before we use it.
- **Engineered, not one-off.** Data moves through a layered pipeline (Bronze → Silver → Gold) with quality checks, version control, and documentation at every step.
